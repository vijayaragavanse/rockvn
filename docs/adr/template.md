## Context

What problem exists today that forces a decision. If this section cannot
name a present-tense problem, the decision fails question 1 and should not
be made yet.

## Decision

What was decided, in one or two sentences, active voice.

## Alternatives considered

Each serious alternative, with the honest case *for* it and the reason it
lost. An ADR with weak alternatives is advocacy, not a decision record.

## Consequences

**Positive:** what this buys us.

**Negative / accepted costs:** what we pay, stated plainly. Every decision
has costs; an ADR listing none is incomplete.

## Interview framing

The two-minute explanation of this decision, written out — question 3 of
the [three questions](../philosophy.md#the-three-questions), pre-answered.
